# Technical Interview Debrief

## General Comments

### Interview Atmosphere
Provide a general explanation of how the interview went. Describe:
- Was it conversational, formal, or casual?
- Was it professional and friendly or tense?
- Overall tone and flow of the conversation

### Interviewer's Attitude
Describe the interviewer(s):
- Name(s) and title(s) if mentioned
- Their demeanor and approach (positive, engaging, direct, etc.)
- How they created the environment for the candidate

Example format:
● The interview was [describe tone/style].
● The interviewer ([name, title]) maintained a [describe attitude] attitude throughout, creating a [describe environment] environment.
● The tone was [describe progression of tone].

## Structure of the Interview

List each section of the interview with approximate time spent:

Example format:
● [Section name] – [Brief description of what was covered]. (≈X min)
● [Section name] – [Brief description]. (≈X min)
● [Section name] – [Brief description]. (≈X min)

Common sections might include:
- Introduction and company overview
- Candidate background discussion
- Technical deep dives
- Coding challenges or exercises
- Behavioral/situational questions
- Candidate questions and wrap-up

## Interview Platform

Specify the technical setup:
- Was it done on the candidate's machine with screen sharing?
- Was it done on an online platform? Which one? (Zoom, Google Meet, CoderPad, HackerRank, etc.)
- Any specific tools or environments used during the interview

## Key Points to Hit

**IMPORTANT**: List the critical topics and skills the candidate must demonstrate to succeed in this type of interview.

For each key point, explain:
- What specific knowledge or skill is required
- Why the interviewer emphasized it (if evident from the conversation)
- Examples of questions or discussions that highlight this point

Example format:
● [Key technical skill/topic] – [Explanation of what the interviewer focused on and why this matters]
● [Key soft skill/approach] – [How this was assessed and what was emphasized]
● [Specific technology or methodology] – [What depth of knowledge is expected]

## Tech Questions

List EACH technology or technical topic discussed in the interview. For each one, include:
- The technology/framework/tool name
- Specific questions asked about it
- Technical concepts or practices discussed
- Depth of knowledge expected

Example format:
● **[Technology Name]** – [Specific aspects discussed: e.g., component creation, performance optimization, etc.]
● **[Framework/Tool]** – [What was asked or discussed about it]
● **[Technical Concept]** – [How it was explored in the interview]
● **[Development Practice]** – [Questions or discussion around this practice]

Technologies might include:
- Programming languages (JavaScript, Python, etc.)
- Frameworks (React, Angular, Node.js, etc.)
- Tools (Git, Docker, CI/CD, etc.)
- Methodologies (Agile, TDD, etc.)
- AI/ML tools (if discussed)

## Situational Questions

List all behavioral, scenario-based, or experience-related questions asked during the interview.

Use direct quotes when possible:
● "[Exact question asked]"
● "[Another question]"

Common categories:
